Net cash per ticket, day by day

Median ticket Middle 50% of tickets Middle 80% of tickets
How to read this: on each day, all simulated tickets are lined up and the chart marks the median and the middle 50% / 80% of their net cash — the lines are cross-sections of the whole fleet, not one ticket's journey. A ticket that finishes (blown or retired) keeps its final net for the rest of the chart, so a line going flat means that slice of tickets is done trading — a plateau at, say, +$3,200 is locked-in profit (payouts banked minus costs), not an account sitting idle. The chart window ends at day 130; longer-running tickets still count in the stats above.
